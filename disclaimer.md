# Disclaimer

nov-cli fetches content from third-party websites by requesting their public
web pages.

- Respect each site's **Terms of Service** and copyright. The bundled
  `gutenberg` and `standardebooks` providers use public-domain works only.
  The `ao3` provider is for **personal reading only** — AO3's Terms of
  Service ask that you not mass-scrape the site, so nov-cli only fetches
  one work at a time, on your direct request.
- Scraping can break when a site changes its HTML; each provider is isolated
  so one break doesn't take down the others.
- This tool is for personal, lawful use. The authors are not responsible for
  how you use it.
